03 Reward Mechanism & Revenue Split

Surfkit operates as an intermediary monetization layer that connects users with third-party advertiser networks, offerwalls, survey syndicators, and background ad delivery channels.

Contractual Revenue Split Formula:
User Direct Reward Allocation: 70% Net Payout
Platform Operational & Defense Fee: 30% Operational Share

3.1 Postback Verification Requirement

Rewards (denominated in SKIT points and credited as withdrawable USD balance at the standard rate of 1,000 SKIT = $1.00 USD) are issued only upon receipt and server-side cryptographic confirmation of verified postback callbacks sent from partner networks to our verified ingestion gateways.

3.2 Cashout Channels & Minimum Thresholds

Cashouts can be requested via PayPal and Direct Bank Wire Transfer once your available balance meets the uniform minimum withdrawal threshold of $5.00 USD for both payout methods. Surfkit processes withdrawals within standard verification windows (typically 24 to 72 business hours).

3.3 Reversals, Chargebacks & Forfeiture

Advertisers and survey sponsors reserve the contractual right to reject or reverse payouts if they detect poor quality, fraudulent responses, speeding, duplicate submissions, or synthetic user agent patterns. If a partner reverses a postback, Surfkit reserves the right to deduct the corresponding amount from your account. In cases of detected fraud, all accrued balances are irreversibly forfeited.

3.4 Referral Rewards

Surfkit provides a flat 10% referral incentive. When a referred friend earns verified rewards, Surfkit credits your balance with an amount equal to 10% of their verified earnings. This bonus is funded from our creator growth allocation and is never deducted from your friend's balance.
