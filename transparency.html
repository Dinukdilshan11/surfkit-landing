Privacy Architecture

Core Transparency Pillars

How Surfkit delivers premium rewards while keeping your digital identity completely protected.

Targeting

Zero History Sold

Surfkit serves relevant monetization offers (surveys, mini-games, and sponsor tasks via our integrated CPX Research partner tunnel, with upcoming support for BitLabs, Lootably, and AdGate) without ever selling or uploading your raw browsing history.

• On-Device Preferences: Category interests remain stored in local Chrome storage. Outbound requests are fully tokenized with zero raw URL payloads.
Local storage sandboxed

Identifiers

Token Cryptography

User sessions are managed securely through pseudonymous account identifiers and randomized cryptographic session tokens.

• Zero Advertiser Tracking: Personal communication channels, email addresses, and real identities are never passed to third-party ad networks or data aggregators.
Anonymous auth tokens

Activity & Postbacks

Edge Handlers

When you surf sponsor ads or complete a partner survey, our secure postback gateway processes incoming reward confirmations with end-to-end cryptographic verification.

• Minimal State Storage: The system records only the transactional event state (e.g., +1 ad view, +$0.60 reward) without logging or retaining personal browsing metadata.
Strict edge verification

Location Data

Country-Level Only

Surfkit only resolves an approximate country-level location derived securely at network edge nodes from your IP address (e.g. United States, Germany, Sri Lanka).

• Inventory Matching Only: Country codes are used strictly to display available local surveys and ad inventory. Exact GPS or residential coordinates are never requested, stored, or shared.
No GPS or Street data

Advertisers & Partner Tunnels

Sandboxed Iframes

All third-party survey and offerwall partners (currently live with CPX Research, with BitLabs, Lootably, and AdGate joining soon) operate within isolated, encrypted iframe tunnels.

• Context Isolation: External partner scripts are strictly barred from accessing your extension storage, session cookies, host tabs, or personal profile data.
Encrypted cross-origin boundaries

Ad Blockers

Full Compatibility

Surfkit is engineered to work seamlessly alongside standard browser ad blockers (such as uBlock Origin, Adblock Plus, and Brave Shields).

• Uncompromised Comfort: You can block intrusive tracking banners across the web while continuing to earn passive USD rewards through Surfkit's dedicated background ad surfing units.
No ad blocker disabling required
